J62 Asset Accounting System

Asset Master

Manage fixed assets and their capitalization details

Purchase Requisitions

Create and manage purchase requisitions for asset procurement

Purchase Orders

Track purchase orders linked to requisitions

Goods Receipts

Record goods receipts for purchased items

Invoices

Manage supplier invoices and payment processing

Process Flow

Asset Master → linked to → Purchase Requisition → linked to → Purchase Order → linked to → Goods Receipt → linked to → Invoice

Each entity in the chain can reference the next entity in the procurement-to-payment process.
